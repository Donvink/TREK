## Innovation (confidential)

字符数：946｜限制：500–1500（含空格）

```text
Residents often spend more time choosing an outing than enjoying it, because ideas, transport, opening hours and personal constraints sit in separate tools. Our core innovation is a feasibility-first recommendation process: it filters curated day trips against a return deadline, walking limits and known conditions before ranking for interests and variety. Existing cards and previously visited places inform the result without turning unverified discounts into promises. The planned difference from generic search or itinerary chatbots is a small set of executable, explained alternatives rather than an unconstrained list. We will test that difference against users' current planning methods. Our proposed lead is a maintained local content base, distribution through resident communities and consented feedback that improves matching. The TREK-based code will follow AGPL; we do not claim a patent or exclusive ownership of upstream software.
```
