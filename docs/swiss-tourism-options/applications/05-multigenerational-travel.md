## Innovation (confidential)

字符数：918｜限制：500–1500（含空格）

```text
Families traveling with parents often struggle to reconcile desired attractions with continuous walking, transfers, rest and luggage. Our innovation is a comfort-constraint model that accounts for cumulative effort and usable rest stops, then explains full and shortened plans. It treats each member's needs privately and distinguishes verified facilities from unknown conditions. This differs from labeling attractions as easy or generating a generic senior itinerary. Optional split-and-rejoin plans would require feasible meeting points and schedules rather than merely drawing separate routes. We will test the model with actual families and compare existing tourism and accessibility services. Our proposed lead is verified local content, sensitive user research and trusted channels, not medical expertise or universal accessibility certification. TREK-derived code remains AGPL; no patent protection is claimed.
```

## Technology & Product (confidential)

字符数：1164｜限制：500–1500（含空格）

```text
This is a design-stage concept using TREK as a possible itinerary and collaboration foundation, not a delivered low-effort travel product. New modules would cover private member preferences, facility evidence, continuous-walking limits, rest windows and shortened plans. Initial scope is one city or region and low-intensity sightseeing; wheelchair-specific and safety-critical needs require separate validation. The six-week roadmap is interviews, on-site checks and manual plans, then preference permissions, constraints, mobile routes and family trials. Medium-term work may add agency tools and verified optional branches. Risks include incomplete facility data, inaccurate effort estimates and sensitive preferences. We will minimize personal information and test privacy, return feasibility and rest availability. Altitude may be shown as a fact and preference; the product will not determine medical suitability. We plan to add an OJP 2.0 adapter to TREK's existing transit module, mapping Swiss journey queries to its common itinerary format for connection and return-time checks. API keys stay server-side; feed access and coverage remain to be validated.
```
