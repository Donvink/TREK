## Innovation (confidential)

字符数：905｜限制：500–1500（含空格）

```text
Destination teams may want to broaden visitor activity beyond a few popular places, while local operators need suitable demand and manageable capacity. Our innovation is an auditable matching and measurement workflow: filter for visitor constraints, use dated operator statements, manage exposure and distinguish recommendation interest from actual visits. Unknown capacity is not labeled as low crowding, and paid promotion cannot override hard needs. We will test whether this improves on static destination lists, campaign placement and manual coordination. The intended lead is cooperative operating relationships, verified local content and credible outcome measurement. At first we will not claim predictive crowd models or causal congestion reduction. TREK-derived code follows AGPL; defensibility must come from maintained partnerships and learning, not unproven patents or control of public data.
```
