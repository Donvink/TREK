## Innovation (confidential)

字符数：959｜限制：500–1500（含空格）

```text
Hotel staff repeatedly translate local knowledge into guest-specific advice, while visitors reconcile separate maps, timetables and attraction lists. Our core innovation is a maintained recommendation workflow that turns hotel knowledge and guest constraints into feasible, explained local plans with alternatives when conditions change. Structured evidence, freshness checks and explicit confirmation distinguish it from an unconstrained chatbot. The staff interface makes knowledge reviewable and reusable across shifts rather than leaving it in individual conversations. We will benchmark against printed guides, front-desk advice and digital guest-service tools. Our planned lead is hotel distribution, verified local content and workflow adoption.
```
