## Innovation (confidential)

字符数：879｜限制：500–1500（含空格）

```text
A family itinerary can look attractive but fail because of steps, missing toilets, meal timing or a child's rest needs. Our core innovation is to treat these execution details as constraints backed by dated facility evidence. The planner fits activities and transport around rest windows and offers a shorter exit plan, instead of simply listing child-friendly attractions. Unverified accessibility or facility availability remains unknown. We will compare the experience with tourism lists, maps, parent communities and generic AI plans. The proposed lead is a locally maintained evidence base, family testing and distribution through suitable accommodation partners. Content quality and update discipline are more defensible than a chatbot interface. TREK-derived code will follow AGPL; we do not claim to own upstream features, hold patents or certify universal accessibility.
```
